KoHs Client Terms of Service

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. What KoHs Client is

KoHs Client is a launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition made by Zymekoh (KoHs). It is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. You need your own legal copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.

2. Use

You may use KoHs Client for free, for personal purposes. You may not sell, redistribute or publish modified versions without KoHs' permission. Third-party components keep their own licences.

3. Your account

Sign-in is done by Microsoft through Windows. KoHs Client never sees your password; game credentials are stored encrypted on your PC and never leave it except towards Microsoft's and Mojang's services.

4. The optimizer

KoHs Client reads your PC and tunes memory, the JVM and game options. It aims for the best performance but does not guarantee any FPS figure. It does not automate actions in the game or give unfair advantages, and it does not change the official launcher's files. Follow each server's rules.

5. Minecraft content

The Minecraft textures and sounds you see in the launcher are read from your own installation of the game, on your PC, and are not distributed.

6. Usage data

Only sent if you accepted on the launcher's first screen, as the Privacy Policy explains. You can change your choice in Settings.

7. Discord status

It is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, you agree that your activity in KoHs Client shows on your Discord profile, as the Privacy Policy explains. Only use it if you are old enough to use Discord, and follow its rules.

8. No warranty

KoHs Client is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, KoHs is not liable for lost data, worlds or games; back up what matters to you.

9. Changes

If these terms change, KoHs Client will show them to you again before going on.

10. Contact

For questions, problems or reports: the KoHs Discord server, https://discord.gg/9t2VxEF7UU.
